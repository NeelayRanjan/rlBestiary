# Assumptions and decisions

- Scope change requested by the user on 2026-10-14: hurry to a pushable MVP. The current deliverable is a tested simulation/viewer/zoo/replay checkpoint with run instructions and a local commit. Designer work, first-generation zoo survival, million-tick trophic/evolutionary studies, final coverage and release packaging remain deferred. This supersedes completing all SPEC milestones before the current handoff; it does not mark those requirements passed or authorize the full v0.1.0 tag.

- The existing local repository had no commits or files; it now uses upstream main at e77d504 as its starting point.
- Rust is installed under ignored `.tools/` because this machine has no Rust installation. `scripts/cargo` selects that toolchain or falls back to the user's Cargo.
- Direct dependency versions are exact and the lockfile pins transitive dependencies; dependencies enter each crate when first needed.
- M0 binary entry points explicitly report that implementation is pending and exit unsuccessfully; they are scaffolding, not completed features.

## Deviations

The M1 performance gate is staged because the named combine trials, world and viewer are assigned to M3, M4 and M8 by the specification. M1 gates measured representative physics throughput; the later budgets remain binding before release.

- M1's throughput benchmark runs nine two-second physics workloads on the 12-part fixture, including actuation, energy accounting and complete trajectories. M3 replaces this representative workload with the nine named combine trials; final release must meet the full combine budget, world budget and renderer budget independently.
- World and viewer budgets cannot be measured in M1 before those systems exist; their measured gates remain mandatory in M4/M8 and M10. No proxy result is reported as evidence that those budgets pass.
- M1 uses SI units, a fixed 1/240-second tick and configurable internal substeps; physical constants and numerical audit tolerances are stored in config/world.ron.

- Genome muscle material genes scale the documented world baselines for contraction range, passive stiffness and damping, so both inherited variation and user tuning affect physics.
- Expansion uses branch-local recursion counts and deterministic depth-first order; a bound truncates that branch without invalidating the remaining body. Routes clipped by expansion bounds are omitted and missing leverage is repaired deterministically.
- Compatibility includes normalized part/joint-count, species-marker and measured-trait distances, with an identity-separation term for otherwise neutral alleles.
- Mutation records include no-op proposals (for example removal at a minimum bound); downstream evaluation records the actual child hash.

- M3 uses two internal physics substeps per fixed tick. The original one-substep fixture passed, but the genome-driven normal creature produced numerical energy false positives in the full harness. Two substeps resolved those without widening the energy tolerances; the deliberate overlapping-body exploit still triggers detection.
- Mass properties are recomputed before the first audited step, so attaching a carry load changes initial potential energy rather than appearing as unexplained generated energy.
- Fluid buoyancy and drag contribute explicitly measured external work to the mechanical ledger. Strike impulse excludes contacts between the target and the floor.
- Replay files use versioned lossless byte run-length compression, a bounded decoder and a content digest. Replaying uses recorded poses rather than cross-platform re-simulation.
- M3 adds the eight authored zoo fixtures for deterministic evaluation tests; their designer integration and first-generation survival checks remain in M9.
- The test profile uses optimization level 2 to keep physical property/regression suites practical; debug assertions remain enabled.

- M4 resource accounting uses integer millijoules and microkilograms so transfers conserve exactly. Nutrients eaten but not retained for growth are excreted to soil; metabolic energy leaves through the dissipated-energy ledger.
- The M4 million-tick fixture uses eight founders on an 8x8 map and a senescence age of two million ticks, because sexual reproduction is assigned to M6. This tests conservation, deterministic continuation and feeding stability before reproduction exists; it does not establish the final population-turnover acceptance result.
- The world performance fixture disables starvation damage for its short measurement window to retain exactly 5,000 agents; feeding, metabolic accounting, brains, plants and movement still run. Ecological survival is tested separately without that override.

- M5's million-tick trophic coexistence and plant/herbivore arms-race acceptance checks are deferred until reproduction is present in M6. A finite non-reproducing prey population cannot validate sustainable predator turnover. Mechanism tests and conservation checks remain required at M5, and the deferred acceptance boxes remain unticked.

- M6 mutations are evaluated synchronously in fixed proposal order, including intermediate mutants that are never born. Existing genome hashes reuse stored traits; asynchronous worker completion never controls birth order.
- Reproductive energy and nutrients are transferred from parents, with gestation loss explicitly dissipated. Designer releases draw their resources from existing soil and plants rather than creating biomass.
- Replay byte caches are derived artifacts, excluded from canonical simulation hashes and stored separately in SQLite. Snapshots retain a replay-cache field for schema compatibility; those bytes are not needed for simulation. The daemon frees successfully persisted replay caches to bound resident memory.
- `config/demo.ron` records the complete seed-42 diversification scenario; `config/world.ron` remains the baseline. Both use the same validated schema and are embedded in created worlds.
- macOS accepted local sockets are explicitly switched to blocking mode before framed I/O; otherwise large snapshot writes can return WouldBlock despite accept-only nonblocking listener configuration.

- Terrain frequency is configurable; the demo uses two waves per axis and a larger sea-level cycle to produce connected land that later fragments. Terrestrial mating requires the same connected land region, and movement integrates across cells at the measured land/swim speed.
- Senescence reduces the maximum attainable health after onset; feeding cannot heal away aging. The regression test verifies death in a fed population.
- M6's typed event stream is consumed by SQLite and exposed over IPC; archive-cell events arrive in M7 and remaining director heuristics in M8. Geographic acceptance establishes a recorded split after fragmentation, not a causal experiment proving fragmentation was the sole cause.
- M6 demo: seed 42, config/demo.ron, 24 founders, 100,000 ticks: 81 living creatures, 11 living species, first fragmentation at tick 11,000. The named acceptance test checks coexistence, descendant species after fragmentation, actual turnover, mutations and conservation.

- M7 uses two bins per specified descriptor and all 128 Cartesian cells as the coverage denominator. This is a conservative bound that includes potentially unreachable combinations; observed discoveries do not change the denominator. Equal-viability elites use the lexicographically smaller genome hash.
- The part-count cut is eight, keeping seven-part founders separate from larger bodies. Seed 42 at 250,000 ticks retains nine species and a 17-part elite, but fills only 13/128 cells (10.15625%); the 15% acceptance remains open.
- Archive reseeding is disabled by default. When enabled, it chooses the least represented elite with deterministic hash tie-breaking and transfers resources from the existing world.
- Profiling showed libm square roots dominated neighbor lookup. Squared-distance comparison followed by normalization of the winner preserves deterministic selection and restores the 5,000-creature throughput gate.

- M8 selection identifies a living creature and its genome together. Historical and never-born genomes remain inspectable, but are explicitly labeled; selecting one clears unrelated living-creature details. An empty world initially shows an archived elite when available.

- Physics audit correction: the stand gate measures the root collider's lower support point and static-terrain contact, rather than its centre height. Stand uses `stand_seconds`; locomotion warms up for that interval and measures net centre-of-mass displacement over `trial_seconds`. This conservative sustained-speed interpretation excludes initial falling and oscillation from useful locomotion. Grounded boxes and the old three-part mutant fail; the seven-part starter still passes.
- The corrected full 12-part combine includes 38 simulated creature-seconds and measured a worst-of-three 0.737 seconds on this build machine, below the two-second gate. Old demo cached traits are legacy evidence and cannot validate the corrected physics.
- Toxicity now has a validated `ecology.toxin_damage` parameter, scales with the amount actually eaten, and applies after healing. Matched clean, susceptible, and resistant feeding scenarios verify selective pressure and exact conservation; they do not establish million-tick trophic coexistence or an evolved arms race.
- Development database schema 2 intentionally rejects schema 1 rather than mixing changed config and physics semantics with old cached traits. The old demo and `var/bestiary-m7-baseline` remain available for legacy inspection/export; new runs use fresh worlds. No production migration is claimed.
- M8 camera clips use recorded animation at a fixed 30 fps over a selected time interval while holding the world snapshot fixed. PNGs capture the 3D camera; interface overlays are omitted. Clip metadata records the tick, genome, trial, start time and frame count.
- The GPU render smoke test is a separate `scripts/check-viewer-render` check because it needs a graphics device/window service. It verifies actual IPC attachment via metadata, nonblank PNGs, and unchanged world hashes; ordinary tests cover the IPC codec and deterministic daemon restart.

- User-requested Evolution reference (2026-10-14): interpret “more realistic” as readable articulated anatomy and muscle deformation driven by existing physical poses; no anatomical organs or locomotion abilities are invented.
- Reference research: [Evolution](https://keiwan.itch.io/evolution), [muscles](https://keiwando.com/evolution/faq/muscles/), [push/pull simplification](https://keiwando.com/evolution/faq/muscles-expand-contract/), [brain control](https://keiwando.com/evolution/faq/network-muscle-control/), [fixed authored bodies](https://keiwando.com/evolution/faq/evolve-body/) and [cosmetic skins](https://keiwando.com/evolution/faq/skins/). Adopt visible attachment-driven anatomy; retain Bestiary's contract-only muscles and evolvable body graphs.
- Viewer anatomy is cosmetic: tapered bones and joint surfaces sit at existing part poses, straight muscles use actual attachment points, radius scales with gene area and inverse square-root length. The collision-shape view exposes physical geometry. Replay files have no activation channel, so replay muscles show deformation without fabricated activation.
- Cosmetic appearance settings live together in documented `config/viewer.ron` (embedded offline defaults; `--appearance PATH` override), separate from reproducible world parameters to avoid invalidating saved worlds or trait caches for a display-only change. No reference assets or source code are copied.

- M8 offscreen QA renders egui into a separate transparent image and composites it after the scene; rendering egui directly into the camera target was overwritten by Bevy's render order. GPU tests now assert the actual interface heading is present, in addition to scene pixels and state-hash preservation.
- The viewer throughput gate uses a 2880x1920 offscreen target, the normal UI and procedural anatomy, 120 warmup frames and 600 measured frames, with GPU completion awaited every frame. This avoids the native 60 Hz presentation cap (59.996 measured fps). The corrected composite measured 73.107 fps with all 2,000 proxies and 20 full bodies in the view frustum on Apple M4 Pro, 24 GiB RAM, macOS 15.3.1; the final release benchmark must be repeated after rendering changes.
- M8 scope reduction under operating rule 13: historical scrubbing covers recorded combine poses, sampled charts, species populations and event-linked genomes. The live world has forward time controls and restartable latest snapshots; full historical world-state rewind is omitted from v1 because retaining and seeking a second world history would substantially extend this milestone. No historical world positions are fabricated.
- Compatible mate search considers eligible neighbors in deterministic distance/ID order instead of rejecting mating whenever the nearest arbitrary creature is incompatible. This changes world trajectories; earlier demo runs remain associated with their original binary and must not be resumed across this change as a same-binary determinism result.

- Anatomy validation: `make check` passed (format, warnings-denied Clippy, workspace tests and release build); `scripts/check-viewer-render` passed actual GPU rendering and attach/detach state-hash checks. Geometry tests cover every zoo preset, valid meshes, muscle endpoints and volume-preserving shortening, collider-mode counts and invalid appearance settings.
- Anatomy throughput on Apple M4 Pro / arm64 at 2880×1920: `target/release/bestiary-viewer /tmp/bestiary-realism.db --benchmark` measured 104.96 fps, with all 2,000 proxies and 20 articulated bodies visible across the measured 600 frames (120 warmup frames). The database was freshly created with seed 42 and 20 founders. This validates the appearance change, not the remaining M8/M10 requirements.

- M8's full-population viewer fixture now creates 2,020 actual agents, so the sidebar carries the population load as well as the 2,000 visible proxies and 20 articulated bodies. It initially measured 55.273 fps; rendering only visible sidebar rows raised the identical gate to 106.935 fps on Apple M4 Pro at 2880×1920. This supersedes the earlier 20-agent sidebar measurements.
- CLI replay export verifies source/genome association, nonlooping 30 fps sampling including the final pose, fresh output directories, completion metadata and every numbered PNG before success. Both recorded-ID and recorded-file GPU tests exported 61 animated 2880×1920 frames from 481 Strike poses without modifying the database or state hash.
- The airborne-gait event requires two completed floor-contact/airborne/landing cycles in a warmed Sprint recording, with every physical collider above `combine.root_clearance` for two consecutive samples, positive measured speed and muscle work, viability and a clean energy audit. This detects repeated airborne phases, not aerodynamic flight.
- A pheromone-guided hunt requires a changed pursuit or attack decision when only pheromone gradients are removed from the same prior recurrent/plastic brain state, actual movement for pursuit evidence, and actual damage to that same victim before the decision expires. Merely encountering a trail is insufficient; evidence and first-event flags survive snapshots.
- Development schema 3 rejects schemas 1 and 2 because it adds persisted milestone evidence and historical first-predation subject IDs. Initial discovery events are saved at tick zero; paused releases flush discoveries without advancing time. Earlier binaries and development databases remain separate evidence.

- Requested coordinated walking uses an authored six-legged founder with articulated hips and knees, alternating inherited oscillator phases, gravity/proprioceptive feedback and terrain-contact feedback; the ordinary evolved motor network produces muscle activation, and only contractile muscle forces move the body. Mutants inherit and can alter these genes; arbitrary evolved bodies are not guaranteed to walk.
- Founder morphology and neural weights live in `config/starter.ron`, embedded at build time and copied into each new genome. This keeps the existing saved-genome/world encoding intact and makes every saved founder self-contained. Force, passive stiffness, friction and metabolic constants remain in `config/world.ron`; display settings remain in `config/viewer.ron`.
- Ground contact sensing excludes self-collisions. Load uses the final solved Rapier interval and its matching timestep (verified against static body weight at one, two and four substeps); slip uses tangent velocity at the actual contact point, including rotation, rather than foot-centre velocity. Joint sensors measure deflection relative to the constraint's rest frame.
- Inspector geometry retains absolute physical positions while the camera follows the root. Its floor matches the arena at Y=0, with a fixed-world grid and optional shadows. Terminal segments retain the collision envelope so a supported corner or edge is visible; world-tier models remain trait-driven proxies and recorded body poses, with frozen animation for paused/offline snapshots.
- Muscle pigment is neutral olive with matching tendons, thinner tapered geometry, correct linear vertex colors and non-emissive activation shading. Replay deformation uses recorded attachment positions; activation is displayed only when actually available from live physics.
- Physics evaluator revision 3 invalidates old combine identities after sensor corrections. New worlds record the revision; compatible-schema worlds with older physics remain readable for historical replay, but writable resume is rejected to prevent mixing stale traits with new evaluations. Earlier schema 1/2 worlds require their corresponding earlier binary; original files and recordings are preserved.
- Reproduce the walking audit with `./scripts/cargo run --release -p bestiary-sim --example gait_probe -- 8 exports/walking-audit`; it records the genome, lossless fixed-step replay and measured gait report, with passive-muscle and prior rigid-limb comparisons printed alongside it.
